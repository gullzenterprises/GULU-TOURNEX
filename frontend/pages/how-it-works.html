The player journey

A straightforward concept for joining an organized tournament.

Discover a tournament that matches your game and format.
Review the schedule, rules, organizer details and entry requirements.
Register through the future platform flow and receive participation details.
Show your participation code to the organizer for verification.
Join the match or bracket and compete under the published rules.
Check the published results and any winnings information after review.
