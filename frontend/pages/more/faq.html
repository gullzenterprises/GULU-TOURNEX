Common questions

Information below describes a product concept, not active services.

What is GULU TOURNEX?

A tournament-platform concept for discovering competitions and guiding players and organizers through a clear journey.

Can I join a tournament from this website?

Not yet. Listings and participation flows are sample frontend content only.

Are login or signup available?

Email/password and Google/Facebook sign-in are available after the owner configures Supabase and the social providers. A new player chooses a unique username after signing in.

Are the leaderboard and winners real?

No. Their names, scores and results are explicitly sample data.

Can I host a tournament now?

The organizer page explains a future process. Tournament creation is not active.

How do I contact support?

Use the contact preview to see the intended form layout; it does not submit messages.
