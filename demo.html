The core flow

Only a visual demonstration for now.

01

Discover

Player finds a tournament, reads details, rules and venue information.

02

Book

Player submits details and completes a demo booking flow.

03

Entry Pass

System conceptually generates a unique 12-character participation code.

04

Check-in

Staff enters/scans the pass and sees the current verification result.

05

Results

Future versions can connect results, prize claims and withdrawal workflows.
